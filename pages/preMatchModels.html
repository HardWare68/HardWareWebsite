Model Results

Before starting a Premier game in Counter Strike, you are presented with a screen that has information, such as:

  • Rank of every player
  • What maps the teams have won on recently
  • Any players that are queued together

My goal was to see if, using that information, it would be possible to predict the winner of a match. Here is a plethora of models trained on the data (see the datasets page for the data!), ranked by their accuracy.

Note the two "dummy" models in there, Uniform and Most Frequent. In order to be considered good, a model should do better than these dummy models.
